OKI's Inn is a guesthouse located in Higashiyama,
Kyoto.

The building is originally a traditional Japanese town house built in the Taisho Era(1912-1926), and still retains the atmosphere of those time.

You can easily walk to center of the city(Shopping area) and also many tourist spot, Kiyomizu Temple, Heian jingu Shrine, Gion(Geisha District).
